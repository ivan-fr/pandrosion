    Understanding the domain and checks

    Points are computed from line and circle intersections. An independent formula then checks the readout. Browser calculations use about 16 significant digits: a displayed zero error does not prove exact equality. Distant centers and tangencies can reduce accuracy.

    On the sphere, a line becomes a circle through the north pole N. Directions at infinity remain distinct in the calculation, even though they map to the same point N. This representation is not a physical protocol with the same construction cost.
