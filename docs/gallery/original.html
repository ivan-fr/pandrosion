Why the triangles are similar

O, B₁, B₂, B₃ and B are collinear. The bases CB, L₁B₁, L₂B₂ and L₃B₃ are horizontal. The segments L₁B, L₂B₁ and L₃B₂ are parallel.

The lengths CB, L₁B₁, L₂B₂ and L₃B₃ therefore form a geometric progression. When L₃ reaches M, the last length is 1: the four lengths are then 2, ∛4, ∛2, 1.

What is actually constructed

Each interior point is computed as the intersection of two lines from its dependencies. No cube root or iteration formula is used to place the points.

The rectangle, midpoints and parallels are standard straightedge-and-compass constructions. Parallels are used here as composite tools; their auxiliary arcs are not shown.
