# V20 → V21: response to the reading report

The central formulas and archived results are unchanged. This is an exposition and reproducibility revision, not an announcement of newly validated hardware.

| Reading-report point | V21 change |
|---|---|
| The geometric purpose is hidden behind formulas. | A motivation section starts from physical realization of operations. The fixed-circle device is introduced through an eight-line cubic example before its general proof. |
| The circle is the main contribution. | It comes first and receives a construction theorem, branch rule, count and self-contained parameter appendix. |
| Notation collides. | Iteration maps use `T`, model coefficients use Greek letters, and support points have descriptive subscripts. The projective denominator is `Q_proj`. |
| The decentered-arc drawing is difficult to read. | Its full circle and complete local support are shown in separate equal-axis views. A degree-seven example separates the readout points. The fixed-circle readout also gets a separate enlargement. |
| Repeated caveats break the development. | Scope and formal coverage are consolidated into one section; branch and nondegeneracy assumptions remain next to the theorems that need them. |
| Lean wording and aggregate source counts overstate scope. | The subtitle no longer advertises a blanket certification. The paper has a module-to-claim table and the README names exact declarations; written analytic steps and the symbolic uniform chart are identified. |
| Relation to prior geometry needs context. | The realization problem is positioned against nomography, Poncelet–Steiner and geometric Halley interpretations, alongside classical Padé/quadratic approximation references. No priority or minimality claim is made. |
| The analog application deserves a concrete explanation. | The actual geometric binary multiplier, centered recurrence and behavioral electrical topology are included, together with retained V30 evidence. It is explicitly the order-three AD implementation. |
| Repository navigation and citation metadata are missing. | A short root reading guide, a V21 reproduction entry point, claim/source links and CFF metadata are supplied. The separate MIT-license PR remains independent. |
| The stereographic section distracts from the paper. | It stays in the gallery; the paper uses planar construction figures. |

The V20, V17 and V30 sources are preserved. Their scripts and records are reused with explicit provenance, rather than copied into a new competing research directory. The existing CI paper check keeps its protected-branch status name while also building V21.

At the author’s request, all five Pandrosion drawings are retained as individual numbered figures: linear MA (with its historical endpoint identified as the current lower-right vertex), AK, centered AD, projective AD of order four, and decentered AD of order five. The linear scalar map and its geometric coincident-state exception are stated explicitly.
