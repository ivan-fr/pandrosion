# V20 → V21: response to the reading report

The component correction formulas and archived results are unchanged. The rewrite improves exposition and reproducibility, and the subsequent safeguarded-circle addition gives a new geometric controller and convergence theorem. No newly validated hardware is announced.

The V20, V17 and V30 sources are preserved. Their scripts and records are reused with explicit provenance, rather than copied into a new competing research directory. The existing CI paper check keeps its protected-branch status name while also building V21.

At the author’s request, all five Pandrosion drawings are retained as individual numbered figures: linear MA (with its historical endpoint identified as the current lower-right vertex), AK, centered AD, projective AD of order four, and decentered AD of order five. The linear scalar map and its geometric coincident-state exception are stated explicitly.

## Geometric safeguard addition

The circle is now paired with a root-free residual gate and centered AD fallback. The new section proves global convergence, constructs the two control products with five joins each, handles unit products and singular circle readouts, and counts initialization, accepted trials and rejected trials separately. A new figure shows the product gadget and rail comparisons. The five original Pandrosion figures remain figures 2–6.

The independent Lean module contains 41 audited theorems, including the actual incidence-based residual/controller composition. The local fifth-order inverse-function argument and physical trace count remain written arguments with their scope identified. A 144-trajectory, 180-digit geometric campaign reaches degree one million; it is not a uniform finite-precision guarantee.
