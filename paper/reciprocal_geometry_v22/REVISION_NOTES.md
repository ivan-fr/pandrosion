## Precision of the resulting claims

The report's proposed “first globally formalized order-five result” is not used as a blanket description. The branch module proves a strict one-step error inequality under branch hypotheses. V22 supplies the invariant-band and branch-driven switching convergence arguments in writing; the concrete inverse-function/local-order assembly also remains written. The older residual-gated global iteration has a separate full Lean convergence theorem. These are distinct proof statements.

The sampled ratio near 0.36 is not promoted to a uniform theorem or a proof of the one-half gate. The branch-driven protocol can omit that gate because strict contraction plus compactness proves convergence, not because a stronger contraction constant has been established.

The analog discussion now interprets the existing campaign sizes and times. It makes no new electrical-performance, priority or minimum-trace claim. V20, V21 and V30 remain accessible as archived companions.
